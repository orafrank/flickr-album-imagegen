## `editorial-handdrawn`

In the bottom half, reinterpret the most recognizable subject, silhouette, gesture, objects and narrative relationship as a highly simplified handmade paper-cover illustration. Use delicate imperfect lines, two to four restrained flat acrylic-like colors sampled from the photograph, rough pale paper, visible brush marks and organic edges. Keep the illustrated subject small and carefully composed, occupying about 10–20% of the lower half, surrounded by abundant negative space. Add only minimal editorial typography when verified and appropriate. Quiet, poetic, refined independent-publication aesthetic. Avoid crayon, bleeding watercolor, realistic rendering, 3D, glossy digital art, commercial cartoons, templates, clutter and excessive text.
